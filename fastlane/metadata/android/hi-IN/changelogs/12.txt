Naya app icon.

Purana icon Google ka standard shield tha, wahi symbol jo doosre firewalls bhi istemal karte hain. Naya nishan ek shield hai jisme ek channel hai: laal unit band sire par ruk gayi, hari unit doosri taraf nikal gayi. App ek naam ki query ke saath yahi karta hai.

Ab yeh sahi adaptive icon bhi hai, isliye gol mask ise nahin kaatta, aur Android 13 aur uske baad ke liye themed version hai.
