A no-root Android firewall with tracker blocking, DNS-over-HTTPS, and connection logging. You choose which apps get online and which trackers get shut down.

No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No data collection. No external servers. Everything runs locally on your device.

HOW IT WORKS

Firewall creates a local VPN on your device. All network traffic routes through it. Apps you have not whitelisted hit a dead end. Apps you allow bypass the VPN and connect normally. The VPN is entirely local - no traffic leaves through a remote server.

FEATURES

Per-App Firewall
- All apps blocked by default - whitelist only what you trust
- Separate Wi-Fi and mobile data toggles for each app
- Block system apps and OEM bloatware
- Changes apply within a second, no restart needed

Tracker and Ad Blocking
- Intercepts DNS queries and blocks known trackers, ads, and fingerprinting
- Bundled default blocklist active out of the box
- Download community blocklists (HaGeZi, OISD, 1Hosts, Steven Black, AdGuard)
- Add custom blocked domains and whitelist false positives

DNS over HTTPS
- Encrypt all DNS queries so your ISP cannot see what you visit
- Choose from Cloudflare, Google, or Quad9

App Lock
- Require a PIN to open the app
- Protects rules from being changed by someone with device access
- Brute-force lockout after wrong attempts

Backup and Restore
- Export rules, domains, and settings to JSON
- Restore on the same or different device

Auto-Start on Boot
- Restarts automatically after reboot or app update
- Works before device unlock (direct boot aware)

Quick Settings Tile
- Toggle from the notification shade
- Respects App Lock when enabled

Connection Logs
- Real-time log with app attribution (Android 10+)
- Filter by status, search by app or domain
- Export to CSV

Traffic Stats
- Per-app and global download/upload totals
- Top apps ranked by traffic volume

PRIVACY

Zero data collection. No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry. The only outbound connections are DoH queries (when enabled) and blocklist downloads (when you tap download).

COMPATIBILITY

Android 6.0 through Android 16. No root required. Works on all devices.
