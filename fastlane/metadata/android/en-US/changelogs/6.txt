What's new in v1.6:

- Major battery fix: the firewall no longer keeps your CPU awake or rebuilds the tunnel on every network change.
- Blocking fixes: rule changes always apply now, Wi-Fi rules are detected correctly, and apps can no longer slip through via a shared app identity.
- Logs now show which app made each connection and the packet size - spot data leaks at a glance.
- Filter logs by Blocked, Allowed, or Trackers, and export everything to CSV.
- Fresh dark design.
