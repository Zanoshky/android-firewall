A new icon.

The old one was Google's stock shield, the same glyph several other firewalls use. The new mark is a shield with one channel through it: a red unit stopped at the closed end, a green one already past the far edge. That is what the app does to a lookup.

It is a proper adaptive icon now, so a round mask no longer cuts into it, and there is a themed version for Android 13 and later.

Nothing else changed in this release.
